gcalcli personal

A private, single-user tool that lets one person read and add events in their own Google Calendar from a personal AI assistant running on their own computer.

What it does

  • Reads today's and upcoming events from the owner's Google Calendar to build a short morning summary.
  • Adds new events to the owner's Google Calendar when the owner asks for it.

Who uses it

Only the developer, with the developer's own Google account. The app is not offered to other people.

Google user data

Calendar data stays on the owner's own computer and is used only to show the owner their schedule. See the Privacy Policy for details.
